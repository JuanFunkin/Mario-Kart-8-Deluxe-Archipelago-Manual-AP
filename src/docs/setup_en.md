# Setup Guide: Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (Manual)

## Requirements
- Archipelago installed.
- Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (base game). No mod is needed.

## Installation
1. Copy the `.apworld` file into the `custom_worlds` folder of your Archipelago install.
2. Edit the included YAML: set your player name in `name` and adjust the options.
3. Put the YAML in your `Players` folder.

## Playing
1. Open **Manual Client** from the Archipelago Launcher and enter the server and your slot name.
2. All checks appear automatically. When you complete one in the game, mark it in the client.
3. When you finish your goal, mark the matching `Goal - ...` location.
